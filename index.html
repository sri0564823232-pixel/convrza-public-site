Installation guide

From purchase to live in one focused setup.

We complete the configuration with the customer, then their web team adds a single secure script before the closing </body> tag.

  1. 1
    Choose the experience

    Select the character, launch languages, colours, welcome message and human handover route.

  2. 2
    Approve the knowledge

    Share the website, FAQ pages and approved documents. We configure the assistant to answer only from these sources.

  3. 3
    Authorize the domain

    We add the exact customer domain to the Convrza allowlist and provide a unique agent ID.

  4. 4
    Paste one script

    The web team adds the supplied line once. No model keys, private prompts or CRM credentials are placed on the customer website.

  5. 5
    Test and launch

    Test text, voice, Arabic and lead capture together; then make the assistant visible to visitors.
